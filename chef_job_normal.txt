EXECUTIVE CHEF
Company: Lumière Fine Dining Restaurant
Location: San Francisco, CA (On-site)
Employment Type: Full-Time
Salary Range: $75,000 - $95,000 per year + performance bonuses
Experience Level: Senior Level

ABOUT THE RESTAURANT
Lumière is an acclaimed fine dining establishment in the heart of San Francisco, known for our innovative French-Mediterranean fusion cuisine. With a focus on seasonal ingredients and artistic presentation, we have earned numerous accolades including a Michelin Star and AAA Four Diamond Award. We are seeking an exceptional Executive Chef to lead our culinary team and continue our tradition of excellence.

JOB DESCRIPTION
We are looking for a highly skilled Executive Chef with extensive experience in fine dining to oversee all aspects of kitchen operations. The ideal candidate will be a creative culinary leader with expertise in French and Mediterranean cuisines, strong leadership abilities, and a passion for creating memorable dining experiences.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Kitchen Operations & Leadership:
• Lead and manage all kitchen operations for 180-seat upscale restaurant
• Supervise and mentor culinary team of 15+ staff including sous chefs, line cooks, and prep cooks
• Develop daily work schedules and coordinate kitchen staff assignments
• Ensure consistent food quality and presentation standards across all dishes
• Maintain high standards of food safety, sanitation, and HACCP compliance
• Conduct regular kitchen team meetings and training sessions

Menu Development:
• Design and execute seasonal menus featuring French-Mediterranean fusion cuisine
• Create innovative dishes using locally-sourced, sustainable ingredients
• Develop tasting menus and chef's specials showcasing culinary creativity
• Collaborate with sommelier on wine pairings for menu items
• Research food trends and incorporate modern techniques into menu offerings
• Recipe development, testing, and documentation for consistency

Food Cost & Budget Management:
• Manage kitchen budget of $900K+ annually
• Control food costs and maintain target food cost percentages (28-32%)
• Negotiate contracts with food suppliers and vendors
• Monitor inventory levels and implement efficient ordering systems
• Minimize food waste through proper inventory management
• Analyze menu profitability and adjust pricing as needed

Quality Control:
• Oversee food preparation and ensure proper cooking techniques
• Taste and inspect all dishes before service
• Maintain consistency in portion sizes and plating presentation
• Implement quality standards for ingredient sourcing
• Conduct regular kitchen equipment maintenance and cleanliness inspections

Special Events & Catering:
• Plan and execute private dining events and catering functions
• Create customized menus for special occasions and VIP guests
• Coordinate with front-of-house for seamless event execution
• Participate in community culinary events and restaurant promotions
