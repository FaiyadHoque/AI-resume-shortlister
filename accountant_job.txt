Job Title: Senior Accountant

Company: Premier Financial Solutions Inc.
Location: New York, NY
Employment Type: Full-time
Experience Level: 3-5 years

Job Description:
Premier Financial Solutions Inc. is seeking a detail-oriented and experienced Senior Accountant to join our growing finance team. The ideal candidate will have strong analytical skills, exceptional attention to detail, and the ability to work in a fast-paced environment.

Key Responsibilities:
- Prepare and analyze monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements in accordance with GAAP
- Manage general ledger activities including journal entries, account reconciliations, and month-end close procedures
- Perform bank reconciliations and cash flow analysis
- Assist in budgeting and forecasting processes
- Prepare and file tax returns and ensure compliance with federal, state, and local tax regulations
- Conduct variance analysis and provide detailed financial reports to management
- Support external audits by providing necessary documentation and explanations
- Maintain fixed asset records and perform depreciation calculations
- Process accounts payable and receivable transactions
- Implement and maintain internal financial controls and procedures
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to improve financial processes

Required Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field
- CPA certification required (or actively pursuing)
- 3-5 years of progressive accounting experience
- Strong knowledge of Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP)
- Proficiency in accounting software (QuickBooks, SAP, Oracle, or similar)
- Advanced Microsoft Excel skills (pivot tables, VLOOKUP, financial modeling)
- Experience with ERP systems
- Strong understanding of tax regulations and compliance requirements
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities
- Superior attention to detail and organizational skills

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience in corporate accounting or public accounting firm
- Knowledge of SOX compliance requirements
- Experience with financial reporting systems
- Familiarity with data analytics tools
- Previous supervisory or mentoring experience

Technical Skills:
- QuickBooks Enterprise
- Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, Word, PowerPoint)
- SAP or Oracle ERP systems
- Tax preparation software
- Financial reporting tools

Soft Skills:
- Excellent written and verbal communication
- Strong time management and ability to meet deadlines
- Team player with collaborative mindset
- Professional integrity and ethical conduct
- Ability to handle confidential information

What We Offer:
- Competitive salary range: $70,000 - $90,000 annually
- Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance
- 401(k) matching program
- Professional development and CPA exam support
- Flexible work arrangements
- Paid time off and holidays
- Performance bonuses
