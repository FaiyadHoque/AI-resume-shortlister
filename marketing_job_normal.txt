MARKETING MANAGER
Company: GlobalTech Innovations

Salary Range: $85,000 - $115,000 per year

ABOUT US
GlobalTech Innovations is a fast-growing technology company specializing in cloud-based business solutions. With over 500 employees worldwide and clients in 30+ countries, we're revolutionizing how businesses manage their operations. We're looking for a talented Marketing Manager to join our dynamic team and help us scale our brand presence globally.

JOB DESCRIPTION
We are seeking an experienced Marketing Manager to lead our marketing efforts and drive brand awareness, customer acquisition, and revenue growth. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in digital marketing, excellent leadership skills, and a proven track record of developing successful marketing campaigns.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Strategy & Planning:
• Develop and execute comprehensive marketing strategies aligned with business objectives
• Create quarterly and annual marketing plans with clear KPIs and measurable goals
• Conduct market research and competitive analysis to identify growth opportunities
• Define target audience segments and develop buyer personas

Digital Marketing:
• Manage all digital marketing channels including social media, email, SEO, SEM, and content marketing
• Oversee paid advertising campaigns across Google Ads, Facebook, LinkedIn, and other platforms
• Optimize website content and landing pages for conversion rate improvement
• Implement marketing automation strategies to nurture leads through the sales funnel

Content & Brand Management:
• Develop compelling marketing content including blog posts, whitepapers, case studies, and videos
• Ensure brand consistency across all marketing materials and channels
• Collaborate with design team to create engaging visual content
• Manage company blog and social media presence

Team Leadership:
• Lead and mentor a team of 3-5 marketing specialists
• Coordinate with sales, product, and customer success teams
• Manage external agencies and freelance contractors
• Foster a creative and results-driven team culture

Analytics & Reporting:
• Track and analyze marketing performance metrics using Google Analytics, CRM, and other tools
• Create monthly and quarterly reports on campaign performance, ROI, and key metrics
• Use data insights to optimize campaigns and improve marketing effectiveness
• Present marketing results and recommendations to executive leadership

Campaign Management:
• Plan and execute product launch campaigns
• Develop email marketing campaigns with segmentation and personalization
• Coordinate events, webinars, and trade show participation
• Manage customer retention and re-engagement campaigns
